Toledo Day Trip

A captivating day trip to Toledo, a UNESCO World Heritage city where history, culture and stunning architecture come together in one unforgettable visit.

Includes:

  • Guided tour of Toledo’s city center
  • Round-trip transfer from the hotel to Toledo
  • Free time for lunch and to explore the center on your own
  • One Kaptiva Sports representative with the group

Date: November 24th, 2026  ·  Price: First 50: $100 $120

Segovia Day Trip

A magical day trip to Segovia, a UNESCO World Heritage city renowned for its Roman aqueduct, fairy-tale Alcázar and breathtaking Gothic cathedral. Discover centuries of history as you walk through its winding streets.

Includes:

  • Guided tour of Segovia’s city center
  • Panoramic sightseeing
  • Walking tour of the city center
  • Round-trip transfer from the hotel to Segovia
  • Free time for lunch and to explore the center on your own
  • One Kaptiva Sports representative with the group

Date: November 23rd, 2026  ·  Price: First 50: $100 $120
