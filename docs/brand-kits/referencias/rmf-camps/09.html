Important Information

During the Camp

 

👕

Uniform Information

Camp Uniform is mandatory EVERY DAY to be able to play. Wear soccer cleats daily.
 

💼

Lost & Found

If an item is lost, inform one of our Team Members and they will guide you to the lost and found station.

☀️

Apply Sunscreen

Very important to apply sunscreen at home, so it allows time to activate correctly by the start of the training. Bring extra sunscreen to reapply through the day. Be careful with applying on forehead as it may drip into eyes with sweat.
 

⛅

Weather

No refunds will be given in case of rain, lightning, tornadoes, tropical storms, hurricanes, wildfires, fires, smoke or hazardous air-quality conditions, extreme heat, weather alerts, natural disasters, or any other natural occurrence or emergency condition beyond our control, as stated in the Parent Agreement.

🍏

Bring Snack and lunch

All participants must bring their own snack and lunch every day. In addition, please bring your own water bottles to refill. Water is provided to all participants at camp water stations (iced cold water will be provided). Please, label all items with first and last names.
 

📋

Evaluation

Please note that coaches will assess all players during the camp, and some participants may receive invitations to exclusive Real Madrid Foundation events based on these evaluations. However, there will be no grade reports provided at the end of the week.
