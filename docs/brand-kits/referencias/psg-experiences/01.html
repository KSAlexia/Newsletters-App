Your week, day by day

Nov 22

Arrival

•  Kaptiva Sports check-in, approx. 2:30–3:30 PM in the lobby of the reference hotel — welcome pack and players' kit handed out

•  Welcome meeting, approx. 4:00–5:00 PM

•  Airport transfers are not included in this pack

Nov 23–27

Training

•  Players travel by bus from the reference hotel to the training facilities with our staff

•  Four days of double training sessions. Players are transported from the reference hotel to their morning session, spend the day with their team, and return to the hotel after the second training session for parent pick-up.

•  Training sessions are closed to relatives, except Parent's Day. The exact date will be confirmed closer to the event

Nov 28

Departure

•  Airport transfers are not included in this pack

•  The reference hotel will be located in the La Défense district. Families arrange their own accommodation

Meals included

•  Relatives: no meals included
