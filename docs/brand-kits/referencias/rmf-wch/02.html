A City Full of History

Madrid began as the settlement of Mayrit in the 9th century. Today, Spain's capital combines centuries of history with lively streets, world-famous art and football passion.

Don't miss: the Royal Palace, Plaza Mayor, Gran Vía and the green spaces of Retiro Park.

 

A landmark to find

Puerta del Sol

Look for the Kilometer Zero marker and the Bear and Strawberry Tree statue.

 

Art & nature

Retiro & the Prado

The Retiro Park and Paseo del Prado are part of a UNESCO World Heritage cultural landscape.

For art lovers

The Prado, Reina Sofía and Thyssen-Bornemisza museums form the city's famous Art Walk. The included city tour offers an opportunity to see some of Madrid's highlights.

A Taste of Madrid

 

Madrid is best explored one bite at a time. Around Plaza Mayor, try a bocadillo de calamares; for a cosy meal, discover cocido madrileño, a hearty chickpea stew.

And for a sweet break, churros con chocolate are always a favourite.

Savoury favourites

Madrid classics

Patatas bravas, tortilla española, jamón ibérico and huevos rotos.

 

A special stop

Mercado de San Miguel

A historic food market beside Plaza Mayor, known for Spanish tapas and regional flavours.

Did you know?

Paella originally comes from Valencia, not Madrid. The capital has its own traditional specialities worth discovering.

Madrid in March

In March, Madrid can have cool mornings and evenings, pleasant afternoons and occasional rain. Conditions can change over the course of a day.

Pack layers, a warm jacket, a raincoat and comfortable walking shoes. Check the forecast shortly before travelling.

 

Morning & evening

Dress in layers

Keep a jacket handy for cooler hours.

 

On the move

Comfort first

Comfortable shoes and a light waterproof layer will help you explore.

More to Discover

A little Madrid trivia

The city never stands still: Gran Vía is famous for its theatres, architecture and shopping. Madrid is also home to a centuries-old tradition of gathering in its plazas for food, conversation and celebrations.
